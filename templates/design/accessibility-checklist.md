# Accessibility checklist: <surface>

- [ ] Semantic structure and heading order are valid.
- [ ] Every action is keyboard reachable.
- [ ] Focus order matches the task.
- [ ] Focus is visible and distinct from selection.
- [ ] Escape closes reversible overlays and restores focus.
- [ ] Screen-reader names, values, states, actions, and announcements are meaningful.
- [ ] Status is not communicated by color alone.
- [ ] Light, Dark, and Increased Contrast remain legible.
- [ ] Reduced Motion and Reduced Transparency preserve comprehension.
- [ ] Text expansion or 200% zoom keeps controls available.
- [ ] Touch and pointer targets meet the platform adapter.
- [ ] Empty, loading, error, and completion states are announced.
- [ ] Automated, keyboard, screen-reader, rendered, and live evidence are reported separately.
